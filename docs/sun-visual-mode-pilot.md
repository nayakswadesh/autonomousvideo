# Sun visual-mode pilot (v4 approved script, chapter 1)

This is a planning-only comparison. It did not change the approved Sun v4
visual plan, generate images, render video, or send a Telegram review.

The existing planner offered image-led scenes but compared an unlabeled still
against a complete code-rendered treatment. The revised selection contract
requires Luna to describe **both complete options** for every ordinary beat:
one focal image with motion and up to two timed cues, and the best eligible
designed treatment with its explanatory relation. A code choice is valid only
when that relation is essential and the complete image option cannot carry it.
The fixed closing treatment is exempt. No image quota was added.

| Chapter 1 beat | Approved v4 plan | Isolated revised-plan probe |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1, opening | generated image | generated image |
| 2, isotope method / early solid | process/working | authentic-image candidate |
| 3, age and scope | metric_reveal | authentic-image candidate |
| 4, sample to reconstruction evidence | juxtaposition | generated-image candidate |
| 5, formation stages and limits | process/working | synthesis/mosaic |
| 6, uncertain birthplace and trigger | synthesis/mosaic | juxtaposition |
| 7, present-day Sun | authentic-image candidate | authentic-image candidate |

The pilot moved from **2 image-led / 5 designed** beats to **5 image-led / 2
designed** beats. It used the approved narration and the existing
`gpt-6-luna` partition and selection calls. No image-generation call was made.

The result supports the narrower finding that the new choice contract changes
planning behavior. It does **not** prove that the five proposed image shots
will look better. In particular, beats 2–4 all center on meteorite material;
their compositions and adjacent-shot variety need review before any full
replan or media generation. The rest of the film was not probed, and the
approved v4 visual plan remains pending human review.
